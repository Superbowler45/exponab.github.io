Why work with Exponab?

Produce-first mindset

Our focus starts at the farm. We work directly with growers, pack houses and aggregators to secure consistent quality and reliable volume in fruits and vegetables.

Dubai as a trade hub

Operating from Dubai gives us access to efficient ports, free zones and world-class logistics infrastructure, enabling smooth movement of cargo.

Transparent operations

Clear documentation, structured contracts, and regular updates at every stage of the shipment keep both suppliers and buyers informed.

Product categories we focus on

Exponab currently focuses on food trading categories centred around fresh, high-demand agricultural goods. For the initial stage, we are prioritising:

  • Category 04 – Fruits and vegetables (including mushrooms, roots & tubers, fresh pulses and legumes, nuts and seeds, etc.)
  • Category 06 – Cereals and cereal products (selected items to support our fresh produce portfolio)
  • Category 100 – Standardised food products that complement our main lines.

Categories such as meat, fish, eggs and ready-to-eat items may be added later as the business scales, depending on demand and regulatory approvals.
