About Exponab

Exponab is a Dubai-based trading and operations company focusing on the international movement of fresh fruits and vegetables and complementary food products.

Who we are

Exponab was formed with a simple vision: create clean, transparent trade flows between reliable producers and serious buyers. Starting with fruits and vegetables, our mission is to become a trusted partner on both sides of the supply chain.

From farm selection and quality checks to documentation and shipment coordination, we stay actively involved in each stage of the process. Our operations leverage Dubai’s strategic position as a global logistics hub, enabling fast and efficient routing to multiple markets.

Our focus

  • Fresh fruits and vegetables as our core product line
  • Selected cereals, pulses and other standardised food products
  • Long-term partnerships with growers, suppliers and distributors
  • Structured supply programmes over one-off spot trades

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become a dependable bridge between farms and markets worldwide for fresh produce and agricultural goods.

Mission

To create value for trade partners through reliable sourcing, quality assurance, efficient logistics, and transparent documentation in every shipment.

Values

Integrity, consistency and long-term relationships define how we work with growers, buyers and logistics partners across the supply chain.
