Our Services

From sourcing to shipment, Exponab manages the full trade cycle for fresh fruits, vegetables and selected complementary food products.

Global sourcing of fresh produce

We identify and develop suppliers for fruits, vegetables and selected cereals/pulses, aligning crop calendars with buyer demand and seasonal windows.

  • Farm and pack house selection
  • Quality and specification alignment
  • Sampling and product approvals where required

Export & import operations

Our team coordinates key operational steps required to move goods across borders, keeping documentation complete and compliant with regulations.

  • Commercial invoices, packing lists and contracts
  • Health certificates and other regulatory documents
  • Coordination with freight forwarders and customs agents

Market development & programmes

We work with buyers to understand their volume, quality and packaging needs and then match suitable suppliers and origins.

  • Regular supply programmes instead of one-off shipments
  • Price and availability updates by season and origin
  • New product introductions within agreed categories

Current category focus

At this stage, Exponab is intentionally focused on a defined basket of food categories so that quality and service stay consistent.

  • Fruits: table grapes, apples, citrus, bananas, berries, pomegranates and other fresh fruits.
  • Vegetables: onions, potatoes, tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers, leafy greens and other staple vegetables.
  • Selective cereals & pulses: items that support and complement the core fresh produce portfolio.

Categories such as meat, fish, eggs and ready-to-eat products are currently outside our scope and may be reviewed at a later stage of growth.

Who we work with

  • Buyers Importers, wholesalers, supermarkets, HoReCa distributors
  • Suppliers Growers, pack houses, exporters and aggregators
  • Geographies Middle East, Asia, Africa and other selected markets
